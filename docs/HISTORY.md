# History

## The idea and the early code

I began thinking more deeply about blockchain, cryptocurrencies, and the idea of Topazio during a period when I was experiencing depression. I was still learning. The implementation was very small compared with what the idea would later become, but it carried a dream much larger than what I could build then. Creating projects, imagining possibilities, and developing the idea of Topazio were part of that time in my life.

Years later, when I recovered this code, I could see ideas in it that later evolved into Topazio Network. At the time I did not know how far the idea might go. This is a personal recollection based on the creator's account; it does not claim that software or blockchain cured depression.

## What the dates establish

No Git metadata, README, release record, or other dated historical document was present in the recovered project. The source-file modification timestamps range from 27 through 29 April 2025. This supports describing the available files as having been worked on in late April 2025, but timestamps alone do not prove when work began or who last modified them. The evidence and uncertainty are detailed in [TIMELINE.md](TIMELINE.md).

The code was recovered, reviewed, sanitized, and prepared for historical publication on 30 September 2026. This modern preservation work is separate from the prototype's historical development.
